Notes on this data

  • This database spans two different data-collection eras -- Kaggle's nbadb warehouse and ESPN's public API -- reconciled into one continuous record back to the 2000-01 season.
  • Four historical franchises' games are folded into their modern successor's team page: the Vancouver Grizzlies into Memphis, the New Jersey Nets into Brooklyn, and both New Orleans Hornets eras into the Pelicans.
  • The Seattle SuperSonics keep their own separate page -- per the 2008 relocation settlement, the Thunder don't claim that franchise's history.
  • Four 2000-01 playoff series are missing games entirely from the historical record, including a Finals with only one game on file.
  • Alternate officials count too: when a game lists more than three names, only the first three (as originally recorded) are treated as having actually worked it.
