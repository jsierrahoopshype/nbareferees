==============================================================================
4. PAIRED TURNOVER ROWS (the double-count trap)
==============================================================================
Pulling only foul/turnover rows, ordered by game and event number, and
pairing them on (game_id, period, clock).

rows whose text has both 'foul' and 'turnover' : 112719
  paired with an ATTRIBUTED foul at the same clock        34866  ( 30.9%)
  paired with an unattributed foul (pre-2015, presumably)    77345  ( 68.6%)
  no foul row at that clock at all (orphan)                 508  (  0.5%)

Reading: a paired row is the turnover HALF of an offensive foul. It
carries no referee by design, so a parser must exclude these rather
than count them as missing attribution -- and must not count the foul
twice either.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
==============================================================================
* Which of two same-initial officials a colliding form refers to. The
  seasons in section 3 narrow it; overlapping careers need a human call.
* Whether a foul row without a referee is a real gap or the turnover
  half of a pair -- section 4 measures the pairing, and the section 1
  rate should be recomputed with those excluded before it is trusted.
* Whether attribution is complete WITHIN a covered season, or only for
  certain event types -- compare sections 1 and 2b before assuming.
* Nothing here is written to any extract. Design comes next, once the
  numbers above are read.
