Reading this: a game with NO crew is one no official is credited with
anywhere on the site -- it is absent from every referee's game log, from
crewmate counts, and from crew chemistry. The gap is concentrated in the
nbadb-sourced seasons; the ESPN-sourced ones (2023-24 onward) are nearly
complete, which is what shows this is a property of the source rather
than of our extraction.

In THIS run, 80 game(s) with a colliding name-form had no crew on file.
Consequence for any copy that promises completeness: a per-referee game
log is every game ON RECORD, not every game officiated. Wording that says
otherwise overstates what the data can support.
