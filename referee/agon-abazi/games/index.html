This log is what the sources carry, not a complete career record: 2,610 of the 41,618 games in this database (6.3%) have no officiating crew recorded at all, so they appear in no official's log; and a further 1,595 (3.8%) list only one or two officials rather than the full three, so some co-officials are missing. The gap sits almost entirely in the seasons taken from the historical database rather than the recent ESPN-sourced ones, and it is a property of those sources, not a filter applied here.

Crew figures, not individual ones. These are the NBA's published review of the final two minutes of games that were close, play by play. A report exists only where the game was close late, about 31% of games, so a total here mostly reflects how many tight games an official drew. Every graded play names the three-person crew and never which official made or missed it, so nothing here can be read as one official's record. A dash means the league published no report for that game, which is not the same as a game with nothing to report.
